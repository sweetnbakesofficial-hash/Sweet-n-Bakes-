ABOUT US

Sweet n' Bakes is for anyone who loves baking or wants to learn how to make Filipino treats. We aim to make baking easier, especially for beginners who might find some recipes confusing at first. Here, you can explore Filipino favorites, from classics like pandesal and ensaymada to newer recipes with different flavors and styles. Each recipe includes clear instructions, ingredient measurements, and helpful tips to guide you along the way.

Aside from sharing recipes, we also want to introduce people to the different baked goods that are part of Filipino culture. Many of these treats are something we grew up eating or often see in local bakeries, family gatherings, and special occasions. Through Sweet n’ Bakes, we hope to keep these familiar flavors alive while giving people the chance to discover and try other Filipino recipes. Whether you're baking for yourself, your family, or just for fun, Sweet n’ Bakes is here to help you enjoy the process and discover something new.

FAQs

  • Are all the recipes beginner-friendly?

    Not all of them! Some recipes are easier than others, and a few might take more practice. We also have a separate category for beginner-friendly recipes to help you get started.

  • What should I do if I don't understand a step in the recipe?

    No worries! We provide tutorial videos that you can watch while following the recipe. This way, you can see how each step is done and have an easier time making your baked treats.

  • Can I adjust the recipe to make more or fewer servings?

    Yes! You can increase or reduce the amount of ingredients depending on how many servings you want to make. Just remember to adjust the measurements properly so the recipe still turns out well.

  • Can I suggest a recipe that isn't currently on the website?

    Of course! We welcome recipe suggestions from visitors. You can contact us through the information provided below to send us your ideas.

  • What if I don't have all the ingredients listed in the recipe?

    You may be able to use suitable substitutes, depending on the ingredient. Check the recipe notes or baking tips for possible alternatives.

  • I followed the instructions, but why didn't my baked goods turn out correctly?

    Small things like measurements, oven temperature, or mixing techniques can affect your results. Try checking the instructions and baking tips again. Baking takes practice, so do not be discouraged.

  • How should I store leftover baked goods?

    Store baked goods in an airtight container to maintain their freshness. Depending on what you made, you can keep them at room temperature or in the refrigerator.

  • Can I report an error or incorrect instruction?

    Absolutely! If you notice a mistake in one of our recipes, contact us so we can review and correct it.
